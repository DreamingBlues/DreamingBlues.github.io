AI Forecast Validation

Analyzed high-dimensional atmospheric data (1M+ observations per timestep) to validate performance and spectral fidelity of foundation weather models like ERA5. Designed Python ETL pipelines using xarray and h5netcdf into model-ready formats to support ML algorithms. Performed topography and synoptic weather station analysis to create insight-driven visualizations

Hyperspectral Vegetation Classification

This project analyzes hyperspectral vegetation imagery and tests whether a small set of selected wavelength bands can preserve most of the useful classification information from a full 430-band hyperspectral cube. The main goal is to reduce hyperspectral data complexity while maintaining strong land-cover classification performance. The selected 7-band method reduced Random Forest training time by 92.6% (13.6× faster) and prediction time by 65.6% (about 2.9× faster), while accuracy decreased only from 95.3% to 92.7% and mean IoU from 0.915 to 0.879, representing relative losses of about 2.7% and 3.9%, respectively.

Fire Weather and Nocturnal Drying Analysis of the 2020 SCU and CZU Lightning Complex Fires

This project examines the 2020 SCU and CZU Lightning Complex fires, two major lightning-caused wildfire events in Northern California. The SCU Lightning Complex burned 396,624 acres across five counties, damaged 26 structures, destroyed 225 structures, and remained active for 46 days. The CZU Lightning Complex burned 86,509 acres across Santa Cruz and San Mateo Counties, damaged 140 structures, destroyed 1,490 structures, caused 1 civilian fatality, and remained active for 37 days. Together, these fires provide a case study for analyzing how extreme fire weather, nocturnal drying, and fire weather indices relate to large wildfire growth and impacts.

Project DIANA

I contributed as a software developer for Project DIANA, where we built custom UAV software to detect and map gas concentrations in real time. Our mission was to protect endangered species and preserve biodiversity by reducing harmful emissions like nitrogen gas in vulnerable ecosystems. We secured a technology grant to acquire advanced drones equipped with Grove AQI sensors for air quality monitoring. I helped process atmospheric data using Arduino systems, enabling real-time interpretation across wide areas and generating actionable insights for environmental conservation.
